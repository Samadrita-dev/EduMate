================================================================================
                    CAMPUS ACADEMIC ATTENDANCE POLICY (ACAD-POL-04)
================================================================================

1. MINIMUM ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENT
1.1 Every registered student must attend a minimum of 75% of the total lectures, 
    tutorials, and laboratory sessions conducted in each subject during the semester.
1.2 Formula for Attendance Percentage:
    Attendance % = (Total Classes Attended / Total Classes Conducted) * 100
1.3 Students with attendance equal to or greater than 75% are unconditionally eligible 
    to appear for the End-Semester Final Examinations.

2. CONDONATION OF ATTENDANCE SHORTAGE (65% to 74.9%)
2.1 The Dean of Academic Affairs may condone an attendance shortage between 65% and 74.9% 
    strictly on the following grounds:
    a) Prolonged illness or hospitalization, supported by a valid medical certificate 
       issued by a registered medical practitioner and endorsed by the Campus Health Center. 
       Medical documentation must be submitted within 3 working days of returning to campus.
    b) Official deputation representing the College or University in recognized inter-collegiate 
       sports tournaments, cultural festivals, hackathons, or academic conferences (Duty Leave).
2.2 A prescribed condonation fee of $25 per subject applies for approved condonation cases.

3. DETENTION (ATTENDANCE BELOW 65%)
3.1 Students whose overall attendance in any subject is below 65% shall NOT be eligible 
    for condonation under any circumstances.
3.2 Such students will be formally "Detained" in that specific subject, debarred from the 
    End-Semester Examination, and awarded the grade 'FA' (Failure due to Attendance).
3.3 The detained subject must be re-registered as a backlog course in a subsequent semester 
    or summer session.

4. DUTY LEAVE (ON-DUTY PARTICIPATION)
4.1 Maximum Duty Leave permissible is capped at 5 classes per subject per semester.
4.2 Pre-approval from the Head of Department (HOD) is mandatory at least 48 hours prior to the event.

5. MONITORING AND OFFICIAL RECORDS
5.1 Attendance is officially recorded by faculty at the beginning of each lecture.
5.2 Attendance percentages are refreshed bi-weekly. Warning notices are dispatched to 
    students and guardians when attendance drops below 80%.
5.3 Students do not have authorization to directly alter or override attendance logs. 
    Discrepancies must be reported to the course instructor within 7 calendar days.
