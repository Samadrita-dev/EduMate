================================================================================
                    EXAMINATION REGULATIONS & EVALUATION CRITERIA
================================================================================

1. EVALUATION SCHEME & WEIGHTAGE
1.1 Each course evaluation comprises Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA) and End-Semester Examination (ESE):
    - Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA): 50%
      * Mid-Term Examination: 30%
      * Assignments & Quizzes: 15%
      * Class Participation & Attendance: 5%
    - End-Semester Examination (ESE): 50%
      * Written Theory / Practical Laboratory Exam: 50%

2. PASSING CRITERIA
2.1 To successfully pass a course, a candidate must obtain:
    a) A minimum of 35% in the End-Semester Examination (ESE).
    b) A minimum aggregate of 40% across CIA and ESE combined.
2.2 Candidates falling short in aggregate or ESE will receive an 'F' grade and must appear in the supplementary examination.

3. EXAMINATION CONDUCT & MALPRACTICE RULES
3.1 Candidates must occupy their designated seats 15 minutes before the exam begins.
3.2 Strictly Prohibited Items:
    - Cellular phones, smartwatches, programmable calculators, electronic recording devices.
    - Printed or handwritten notes, chits, formulas inscribed on clothing or stationery.
3.3 Penalty for Malpractice:
    - Possession of unauthorized materials results in immediate expulsion from the exam hall, 
      invalidation of the exam paper, and formal hearing before the Malpractice Committee.
    - Serious infractions may lead to suspension for up to two consecutive academic semesters.

4. MAKE-UP EXAMINATIONS
4.1 A student who misses a Mid-Term or End-Semester examination due to sudden illness or unavoidable 
    bereavement may petition for a Make-Up Examination within 5 days of the missed exam.
4.2 Petitions require approval from the Academic Senate and verified hospital discharge summaries.

5. GRADING SCALE (10-POINT SYSTEM)
   - O (Outstanding): 90% - 100% -> Grade Point 10
   - A+ (Excellent): 80% - 89%   -> Grade Point 9
   - A (Very Good): 70% - 79%    -> Grade Point 8
   - B+ (Good): 60% - 69%        -> Grade Point 7
   - B (Above Average): 50% - 59% -> Grade Point 6
   - C (Pass): 40% - 49%         -> Grade Point 5
   - F (Fail): Below 40%         -> Grade Point 0
